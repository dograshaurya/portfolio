The challenge

The product needed reliable transaction handling and a deployment process that could support frequent releases without introducing avoidable operational risk.

What I built

  • Built wallet creation, personal QR codes and wallet-to-wallet transfer flows.
  • Implemented transaction processing, wallet balances and transfer history in Node.js.
  • Used PostgreSQL for financial data integrity.
  • Delivered a PWA experience without requiring an app download.
  • Implemented automated testing and deployment scripts on Linux.
  • Established CI/CD from the start for rapid, repeatable releases.

Outcome

Release time reduced from hours to under 15 minutes
