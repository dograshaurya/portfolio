The challenge

Organisers needed one system to manage events and classes while customers needed reliable live availability and ticketing at the point of entry.

What I built

  • Designed four roles: Super Admin, Organiser, Customer and Ticket Checker.
  • Built event and class management with capacity limits.
  • Implemented live slot availability and customer booking.
  • Generated QR-coded tickets for confirmed bookings.
  • Built a mobile-optimised ticket scanner for real-time validation.
  • Added central administration for users, events, revenue and configuration.

Outcome

End-to-end booking and entry workflow
  • End-to-end booking and entry flow supporting multiple concurrent events.
  • No reported ticket-validation failures.
